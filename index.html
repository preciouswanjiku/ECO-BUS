Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Choose M-Pesa at checkout and enter your Safaricom number. Send the fare to our Pochi la Biashara number 0180 350 905 from your M-Pesa menu. Submit the M-Pesa confirmation code on the site — our team verifies the money landed and releases your e-ticket (also sent to you on WhatsApp).

Yes — the moment your booking is confirmed you get an e-ticket showing your seat number, bus and departure time, plus a payment receipt. Both can be downloaded as PDF, and you can always retrieve them under My Booking using your reference code (e.g. ECO-7K2M9X).

Each passenger may check in two pieces up to 50 kg total (25 kg per piece on long-haul routes) plus one hand luggage. Extra kilograms are charged per route at the terminal.

Yes. Our crew guides every passenger through immigration at Busia/Malaba (Uganda–Kenya), Elegu/Nimule (Uganda–South Sudan) and Nadapal (Kenya–South Sudan). Visa fees are not included in the fare.

Changes are free up to 12 hours before departure. Cancellations made more than 12 hours ahead receive an 80% refund; later cancellations can be converted into travel credit.

Children under 4 travel free on a parent's lap (one per adult). Ages 4–11 pay half fare on Standard, and full fare in Business where seats are individually reserved.

Business class includes a hot meal and drinks. Standard passengers receive bottled water and snacks, with meals available for purchase at rest stops.

  1. Open M-Pesa → Send Money (or Pochi) on your phone.
  2. Send to 0180 350 905.
  3. M-Pesa texts you a confirmation code (e.g. QK71GH8Y9A).
  4. Enter that code below — we verify and unlock your e-ticket.
